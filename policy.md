Privacy Policy - WhatsApp Cleaner
Last updated: April 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WhatsApp Cleaner ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Do Not Collect
WhatsApp Cleaner operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or files from your device to any external server. All file scanning, analysis, and deletion operations are performed locally on your device.

Permissions We Request
Storage / Media Access: Required to scan, analyze, and delete files from your device's WhatsApp media folders. This access is used solely for the App's core functionality and no data is transmitted off your device.
Data Storage
All data processed by the App, including scan results and file metadata, is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any external service.

Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
